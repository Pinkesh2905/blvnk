# BLVNK — photo prompts for ChatGPT image generation

36 colourways (18 sneakers, 18 caps). Each has a **hero prompt** (shot 1). Use the follow-up lines to get the other angles of the *same* product.

## How to get consistent results
1. **One conversation per colourway.** Paste the hero prompt. When you like the result, keep going in the same chat with the follow-up lines below (they start with "Same exact product…").
2. Ask for **square 1:1**. Landscape crops badly on the product cards.
3. If the lettering (BLVNK) comes out wrong, say "Regenerate, the wordmark must read exactly B-L-V-N-K". If it still fails, ask for "no lettering" — the site works fine without it.
4. Name and place files exactly as shown, then run `python tools/build-photos.py`. The site picks them up on refresh. Colours without photos keep the generated renders, so you can do this in any order.
5. **Minimum**: shot 1 for every colourway (36 images). A product page with one photo shows it full-size; with 2–4 it shows a gallery.

## Shared style block (already included in every prompt below)
> Premium e-commerce product photograph, shot on a dark charcoal seamless studio backdrop (#1a1a1d falling to near-black at the edges) with soft directional key light, a subtle orange-tinted rim light from behind, a faint floor reflection and a soft contact shadow. Sharp focus, true-to-life materials and stitching, no props, no people, no watermark, no extra text. Square 1:1, product centred and filling about 70% of the frame.

## Follow-up lines for shots 2–4
Paste one of these into the same chat after the hero shot.

**Sneakers**
- Shot 2 (3/4 front): "Same exact sneaker, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. Three-quarter front view, toe pointing toward the lower right, camera slightly above. Square 1:1."
- Shot 3 (Heel): "Same exact sneaker, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. Rear three-quarter view showing the heel counter, pull tab and collar. Square 1:1."
- Shot 4 (Outsole): "Same exact sneaker, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. The shoe tilted onto its side so the outsole tread and midsole edge are visible. Square 1:1."

**Caps**
- Shot 2 (Side): "Same exact cap, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. Perfect side profile, brim pointing right. Square 1:1."
- Shot 3 (3/4 view): "Same exact cap, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. Three-quarter front view from slightly above. Square 1:1."
- Shot 4 (Top / back): "Same exact cap, same colours and details, same studio lighting and backdrop. View from the back showing the strap closure and rear panels. Square 1:1."
