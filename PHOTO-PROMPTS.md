# BLVNK — photo prompts for ChatGPT image generation

36 colourways (18 sneakers, 18 caps). Each prompt is complete and standalone (guard line + style + subject).
These are the prompts used for the current photos: neutral white studio lighting, varied accent colours, no orange glow.

## Tips
1. **One new chat per colourway.** Paste the prompt, check the result, save it.
2. ChatGPT keeps unsent draft text and can pull earlier designs from chat history into new chats. Every prompt starts with a line telling it to ignore earlier chats; keep that line.
3. Save files as `img/products/<slug>/<colour-index>-1.png`, then run `python tools/build-photos.py`.
4. For more angles, follow up in the same chat: "Same exact product, same lighting and backdrop. <angle>. Square 1:1." and save as `-2`, `-3`.

## Shared style block
> Premium e-commerce product photograph, shot on a dark charcoal seamless studio backdrop (#1a1a1d falling to near-black at the edges) with a soft neutral white key light, a subtle cool white rim light from behind, a faint floor reflection and a soft contact shadow. No orange or coloured glow in the lighting or the background. Sharp focus, true-to-life materials and stitching, no props, no people, no watermark, no extra text. Square 1:1, product centred and filling about 70% of the frame.
